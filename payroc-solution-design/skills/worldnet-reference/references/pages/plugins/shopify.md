# Installation Guide

## Worldnet Shopify App

To enable the Worldnet Payments App for Shopify, please follow the below steps. For specific questions concerning your site development, please contact your eCommerce developer.

## Before you start

- Ensure you have your Worldnet Terminal ID(s).
- The Shopify password that was provided to you on your original installation will be needed for this migration.

## Setup and Install

Step 1. Follow the link [https://apps.shopify.com/worldnet-payments](https://apps.shopify.com/worldnet-payments) and click the **Add app** button

Step 2. Click **Install app**

Step 3. On the login page enter your Terminal Number and Password.

Note: Contact Support to receive your Shopify password.

**Step 4**. Select the Payments methods that will be supported on your website. Click **Activate Worldnet Payments**.

If setting up in a test system, make sure you select **Enable test mode**.

**Step 5**. Verify Payment

That's it, your setup is complete. Perform a production transaction to ensure you receive a successful response.
