# Google Pay™

Google Pay helps users quickly and easily complete purchases in apps and on the web using using cards stored in their Google wallets.

> **Note**
> **Supported OS**: `Windows` `Ubuntu` `Android` `iOS`
> **Supported Browsers**: `Google Chrome` `Safari` `Opera` `Microsoft Edge` `UC Browser` `Firefox`

## Availability

Google Pay is only available for **Elavon** terminals.

When it comes to being able to accept Google Pay, the location of your business matters. Merchants in the following regions are eligible:

- United States
- Europe
- Asia Pacific
- Australia

Customer compatibility depends on where the customer stores their card details:

## Integrating

In this integration method you only a few simple steps:

**Step 01**: Contact our support team, and specify the website domain that you wish to process Hosted Payments from.

**Step 02**: Ask the support team to enable Google Pay for the terminals you wish to process Google Pay Payments through.

After those settings are in place, all your users will see the “**Buy with G Pay**” button option when using a HPP, and once they click it, Worldnet is going to take care of the rest.
