# Integration Docs

The following pages help you to understand the main elements involved in the integration process, providing more details on the integration methods available and how to test your integrations during your development process, using a Sandbox Account.

Don't forget to consult the **Understanding the Integration** option at the **[API Specification](https://developers.worldnetpayments.com/developer/api_specification)** page. There you can understand better about the integration methods and how they are related to Testing your integration.

1. **[Sandbox Testing - Worldnet Gateway](https://developers.worldnetpayments.com/selfcare/integration_docs/sandbox_testing)**: understanding the sandbox testing specifically for integrations of Worldnet Gateway.
1. **[Sandbox Testing - CashFlows Gateway](https://developers.worldnetpayments.com/selfcare/integration_docs/sandbox_testing_cashflows)**: understanding the sandbox testing specifically for integrations of CashFlows Gateway.

1. **[Testing Guide](https://developers.worldnetpayments.com/selfcare/integration_docs/testing-guide)**: general test guidelines and tips to verify your integration before going to any further.
