# Frequently asked questions

#### What e-commerce platforms do you support?

The list with currently supported shopping carts can be found in our plugins section.

#### Where can I find a plugin for my version of shopping cart?

Please contact our Integration Team with your shopping cart version and we will send you the relevant plugin with documentation.

#### How can I test my integration?

You can use our SandBox Test Account or you can ask our Customer Support Team for a full dedicated test account.

#### Do you have any requirements regarding testing my integration?

Yes. Before we will be able to activate your new Live account, we will need confirmation that our Testing Guide has been run through. This is our policy and it is just to ensure that some fringe cases are tested as well as some common web server issues. We don't perform tests on behalf of merchants or developers as it's not feasible or desirable for us to gain the access to merchants' or developers' systems.

#### Where can I find any test credit card details?

Test credit cards details can be located in our Testing Guide.

#### I need information to help me with the integration process

Most information can be found on our Integrator Guide.

If you still need assistance then please contact our Integration Team and we will do our best to help you resolve your problems.

#### Do you have any sample code I could use with my integration?

Yes. All available sample codes can be found in our sample codes section.

If you still need assistance then please contact our Integration Team and we will do our best to help you resolve your problems.
