## PIN Pad Setup

Set up PIN Pad

**Power on PIN Pad**

1. Press power button for three seconds.

To ensure **Bluetooth is enabled** on the PIN Pad

1. Press yellow button.
1. Press 'arrow button down' twice to 'Settings', press 'OK'
1. Press 'arrow button down' twice to 'Bluetooth', press 'OK'.
1. Ensure Mode is “Enabled”. If Disabled, press ‘OK’ to enable.
1. Once enabled, press red button back to start.

Make sure to press to get back to 'home' screen, Bluetooth won't work otherwise.

## Smartphone Setup

**Download and install the app**

1. Go to the App Store (iPhone) or the Play Store (Android).
1. Tap on the Search icon.
1. Enter “Worldnet Payments Mobile”.
1. Select the Worldnet Payments Mobile free application.
1. Install the App.

**Enable Bluetooth**

1. In the phone settings find Bluetooth settings.
1. Turn Bluetooth to ‘On’.
1. Phone should start scanning for the PIN Pad, If not manually scan for device.
1. The PIN Pad name will be similar to the serial number on the back of the PIN Pad, but beginning with ‘PP’.
1. Tap on the name and if asked to confirm a code tap 'OK' or 'Confirm'.

**Setup the Worldnet Payments App settings**

1. Open Worldnet Payments Mobile App.
1. In top right-hand corner click on settings dots for app settings.
1. Tap on ‘Terminal ID’, input Terminal ID sent by email.
1. Tap on ‘Shared Secret’, input Shared Secret sent by email.
1. Once done tap ‘back’ button on phone.
1. You should hear a beep, confirming the Bluetooth connection of the App to PIN Pad and the Bluetooth icon should go blue.
